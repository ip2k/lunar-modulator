# Credits and licences

Lunar Modulator stands on other people's work. This chapter names it and the
terms it is used under.

## Sound engines and effects

- **Mutable Instruments**, code by **Emilie Gillet**, MIT licence: the
  engines of Plaits (Macro, Macro Heavy and Six-Op FM), the macro-oscillator
  of Braids (Shapes), the reverb of Rings (Plate) and Plaits' ensemble and
  diffuser (Ensemble, Diffuse). The code is used unmodified. Lunar
  Modulator is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mutable Instruments, and
  its engines carry names of their own.
- **Six-Op FM's patches** are the three banks Plaits distributes with its
  code. Their origin is not stated there.
- **Schwung**, the plugin interface for the Ableton Move by **Charles
  Vestal**, MIT licence, which Lunar Modulator implements so that Schwung
  modules can run unchanged.
- **Sophie** by **Matt Estela**, MIT licence.
- **PSX Verb** by **Charles Vestal**, MIT licence.

Each engine's credits are also in the firmware itself, generated into the
tables of chapters 5 and 6.

## The sequencer

- **Movy** by **megadake**, MIT licence: Lunar Modulator's sequencer
  reproduces the behaviour of Movy's sequencer core, and its design follows
  Movy's.

## Research this project builds on

- **aroum**: the first analysis of the FM-1's updater and teardown photos.
- **AL-255**: disassembly of the FM-1's firmware, its update protocol and a
  safety review whose rules this project follows.
- **kagaimiq**: documentation and tools for JieLi chips, including the
  recovery mode the dongle uses.
- **probonopd**: notes on a sibling instrument built on the same platform.
- **Google's music-synthesizer-for-android** and the **Dexed** family,
  whose FM engine M-VAVE's firmware is built on.
- **Baud Girl**, whose third-party FM-1 firmware showed how firmware reaches
  users through the FM-1's own update method.

The project's research notes credit each source in detail.
