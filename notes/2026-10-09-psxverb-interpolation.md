# PSX Verb interpolator correction (2026-10-09)

## Finding and source

The `sw-psxverb` half-band interpolator mixed two signal-history representations. It wrote a zero-stuffed stream into `halfband_t.state`, then traversed pre-shaped polyphase arrays at every history slot. Those arrays were not the even/odd split of the production 39-tap kernel: the even list duplicated/misplaced the center-adjacent taps, while the odd list omitted the center coefficient `0.6328125` at raw tap 19. A bounded impulse probe at 22.05 kHz differed from a direct FIR oracle on its first output tick (`-0.004356503` versus `-0.001467466`).

The correction stores one input-rate sample per history slot and applies the actual even and odd raw-kernel taps at input-rate stride. The expected output is independently computed by zero-stuffing into a second history and convolving all 39 taps. The coefficient oracle is from Charles Vestal's MIT-licensed `CVCHothouse/PSXVerb/PSXVerb/Halfband39.h`, commit `b94500f72be70140f3d094d69d0c43d04ce72ecc`; the existing Schwung copy is also MIT. No upstream DSP implementation was copied beyond the existing kernel coefficients. The vendored-source note records the local patch and re-vendoring procedure.
