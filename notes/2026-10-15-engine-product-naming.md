# Lunar engine names and upstream attribution

The current user-facing engine names are Drawbar, Trio and Phase Bend. They are Lunar engines, not product features of Felucca. Documentation now describes them that way in the README, developer overview, engines guide and sound-engine chapter. The guide heading and its internal links use a Lunar/upstream-derived label.

Attribution remains explicit: these implementations derive from Leo Kuroshita's WHEEL, TRIO and PHASE engines, with Hügelton Instruments and GPL-3.0-only provenance retained. The existing credits and research notes still identify Felucca where it is the historical source project. FM6 attribution likewise remains factual.

Current operational references use the renamed integration paths and test/oracle names supplied by the parallel engine-renaming work. Documentation-only validation: diff check and targeted grep for obsolete integration names/paths. No code, binaries, test results or hardware claims changed.
