# FM-1 audio-runtime comparisons for the dev-board campaign

Research snapshot 2026-10-23. This records implementation facts and upstream
measurements, not independently verified flawless playback on our hardware.
No foreign HAL, supply code or binaries have been incorporated in Lunar.

[verified: source inspection] Felucca measures elapsed rendering and increments
a late counter if DMA changes its free half before completion. It includes
nested interrupt cost in its overload decision; two successive blocks above
85% request gradual voice reduction. A lower CPU average alone can therefore
hide both deadline spikes and intentional voice loss. Measure these separately.

[reported: X0X's development note] Rebalancing 808/break work against
909/303 work removed the dropout in its documented stress run. Its clock
investigation found 360 MHz on that FM-1, superseding an earlier 240 MHz
assumption. This is not a clock measurement of our dev board or our FM-1.
[Reported later release changes](https://charlesvestal.github.io/fm1-x0x/new/)
include one-core fallback and stock supply settings after instability on
some units. A worker can add throughput and also introduce hardware faults.

[reported: Melodee build documentation] The resident profile checks the
reachable DSP graph for accidental XIP calls, including indirect engine
callbacks. Its host replay capacity improvements are explicitly not target
CPU-headroom evidence. The documentation also records CPU1 SRAM misreads,
startup-order failures and fallback behavior; some earlier clock-retune
builds failed before display/USB startup. Do not reproduce those experiments
as an incidental optimization. SRAM residency, bounded handoffs and counters
are useful design ideas; supply/clock changes require their own evidence.

[verified: fm1-nes source inspection] Its queue distinguishes startup priming,
normal output, underrun frames and rebuffering. This makes silence inserted
by the transport visible separately from silence generated by an engine.
The internal-DAC dev-board probe is a different output path; a good result
there must still be revalidated through the FM-1's external I2S codec.

## Priority and acceptance

1. Establish continuous low-level internal-DAC output, with callback/full-IRQ
   timing plus actual KA6 capture. Confirm routing and adequate signal level.
2. Integrate a real Lunar engine, then representative mixed engine/effect
   loads. Keep deterministic note/parameter schedules and matching desktop
   references. Record dropouts, voice shedding, render spikes and capture
   defects separately, including UI/control activity when integrated.
3. Compare XIP and deliberately SRAM-resident hot paths on the same workload
   and clock. Include tables and indirect callees, not just the entry function.
4. Only consider dual-core scheduling after single-core costs, memory layout,
   stacks, joins and failure behavior are established. First read/measure the
   actual clock and supplies; do not adopt a community clock assumption.
5. Require the sustained workload/analog acceptance defined in
   [the campaign note](2026-10-23-devkit-audio-performance.md). Passing a tone,
   host replay or isolated resampler does not finish the campaign.
