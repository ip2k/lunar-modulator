# 2026-10-29: 30-minute soak of the eight-voice trunc12 image on the dev kit

The owner's release rule (buffered, 2026-10-28) asks for:
- zero underruns over a 30-minute analog soak;
- both cores' mean load at or below 85% of the block budget (29,604 of
  34,829 ticks);
- the worst block explained;
- the queue latency stated.

This run meets all four on the AC79 dev kit at 320 MHz. It was a dev kit, not
an FM-1: the kit plays through its internal DAC, the FM-1 through ALNK0 to an
external codec (trap 10). Nothing was sent to the FM-1.

## The run

- **Image.** `clk320-soak30/cap8-trunc12`:
  - the same source as the 320 MHz cap8-trunc12 image in
    `notes/2026-10-29-devkit-perf-levers-320.md`, with the preparer's
    `--cycles 57` in place of 3;
  - candidate sha256 `fcf72c87…`, `app.bin` `47b28b34…` (597,916 B);
  - writer margin 3,908 B, `audit_link` PASS [verified].
- **Bench.** bench01, using Codex's protocol:
  - acknowledged UART ROM entry, then an app-only write of
    `0x4000..0x96000` (147 sectors), with no readback and no backup;
  - UART (Tigard, 1,980 s) and KA6 (1,900 s) captures armed before the reset;
  - one reset, never retried;
  - an ordinary PING acknowledged afterwards.
- **Heap.** 5,332 B after DAC open, as predicted.
- **Polyphony.** `LUNAR_POLYPHONY fixed=8 adaptive=0 policy=cap8`.
- **Receipts.** In `scratch/bench-2026-10-29/clk320-soak30-cap8-trunc12/` (not
  tracked), scored with `tools/devkit_pipeline_result.py`.
- **Independent check.** A separate lane re-derived every verdict from the
  raw files.

## Verdict

| Condition | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Zero underruns, ≥ 30 min | **pass** | produced = harvested = submitted = 1,256,964 blocks (1,823.4 s, 30.4 min); fault, backend, DAC late and DAC gaps all 0; 228 of 228 case hashes and the fold `4bf911ba` match the host replay [verified] |
| Producer mean ≤ 29,604 | **pass** | 22,970 ticks, 66.0% (cases 20,369 / 23,098 / 24,208 / 24,205) [verified] |
| Consumer mean ≤ 29,604 | **pass** | 28,848 ticks, 82.8%; 756 ticks under the line; consumer max 30,154, no consumer block late [verified] |
| Worst block explained | **pass** | case 3, 34,978 ticks, `dispatch` 21,112 of them; on the first block of a case segment, with the queue full (ready = 8), so the buffer absorbed it |
| Queue latency stated | **pass** | 8 slots × 64 frames at 44,100 Hz = 11.61 ms; 15.96 ms with the 3-window DMA ring [verified arithmetic] |

Notes on the verdict:
- **Two producer blocks went over 34,829 ticks**, both in case 3: 34,978 and
  34,850. The firmware's old per-block rule therefore printed
  `failure_line=370` and `complete=0`. Under the buffered rule this is not an
  underrun: the queue covered both blocks, and the DAC never ran short.
- **The worst blocks all fall on segment starts.** Each case's worst producer
  block, and both late blocks, has a tag that is an exact multiple of 5,513,
  the case-segment length in blocks [verified]. The cause is [inferred]:
  - Notes do not line up with segment starts, because the 1 s sequencer grid
    drifts 32 frames per segment. So the load is more likely the case and
    program change than a note-on burst.
  - Per-event stage timing would settle it.
- **The minimum queue depth during the run was not measured.**
  - `ready_min` (1) is sampled before the acquire, and the end-of-run drain
    forces it to 1 in every run, so it does not show the margin.
  - Measuring it needs a firmware change (sample only while produced < total)
    and a new run.

## Analog screen of the whole capture

Screening only, not acceptance.

- **Music span.** 5.795 s to 1,829.263 s of a 1,900 s capture; peak
  −15.4 dBFS. The DAC-open transient (−9.2 dBFS at 3.8 s) and a preroll pop at
  5.53 s come before the music.
- **Clean on every detector:**
  - no runs of 16 or more exact-zero samples;
  - no 5 ms frame below −60 dBFS inside the music;
  - no case segment more than 3 dB off its case median (standard deviation
    0.05–0.14 dB per case over 57 cycles);
  - no lost or inserted block on the beat grid.
- **Calibration.** A planted 16-block dropout was caught.
- **Limits:**
  - A single lost block (about 70 samples) cannot be told from a quiet
    passage by level alone; the receipts and the beat grid cover that case.
  - The screen does not judge whether the audio sounds right.
- **Rate.** About 44,117 Hz against the KA6 time base [inferred].

## What this does not cover

- An FM-1. The kit runs at 320 MHz on its internal DAC; the FM-1 runs at
  360 MHz into an I2S codec. The owner chose (2026-10-29) to estimate
  360 MHz by scaling.
- Listening. The excerpts were A/B'd for the sine, but this soak was not
  listened to.
- Workloads other than the pipeline's mixed cases.
