# 2026-11-10 — M5 on the FM-1: the image loaded and was started, then the chip reset to V15

Milestone M5 of docs/18, run by the runbook docs/19 on the owner's FM-1 with
the owner at the bench. **Result: the RAM load and the jump worked; the image
showed nothing.** About 3.9 s after the jump the FM-1 reset and booted stock
V15. No test pattern, no LED blinks. The flash was identical before and after
the run, and the unit identifies as `FM-1_015` afterwards.

## What it answers (docs/19 §6)

- **The power switch cuts the SoC fully with the battery in.** With the
  FM-1 off, the transporter reported `uboot=0 v15=0` (steps 8 and 14), and
  the next power-on came up in the ROM again [verified].
- **The transporter after an unanswered `jump`:** the ROM's USB device
  stayed attached until the chip reset, 3.89 s after the jump; the
  transporter then fell back to host mode and waited. `status` worked
  normally afterwards [verified].
- **Whether the image runs:** not settled. The image never drew or blinked.

## Why it showed nothing: candidates, all [inferred]

`m5.bin` turns the backlight on (PA2 low) right after confirming core 0, then
binds TIMER4, configures SPI1, draws the pattern, and only then starts the
blink loop. So "no pattern and no blinks" means it stopped before the blink
loop or never started; the dark TFT suggests it also never reached the
backlight, although a lit backlight over a black panel is hard to tell from
off.

1. **The watchdog.** The cold-entered ROM may leave the watchdog armed, and
   `m5.bin` never clears it (docs/19 step 13 flagged this). A reset 3.9 s
   after the jump fits a ~4 s watchdog period. But a watchdog alone would not
   stop the image drawing in its first few hundred milliseconds, so this
   explains the reset, not the blank screen.
2. **A fault or hang before the backlight.** The jump argument, the stack
   placement against the ROM's work RAM (docs/19 §6), or the core check
   (`cnum`) could fail on the FM-1's ROM entry state. A hang would then be
   ended by the watchdog (1).
3. **A hang in TIMER4 or SPI1 binding** with the backlight on but the
   panel never drawn, then the watchdog.

Whatever it was, a chip reset from the ROM-entered state booted the
application from flash, not the ROM again: the reset was a full system
reset, and the stock boot path took over [verified: V15 screen, identity].

`lunar_m5_status` in SRAM would say which step it reached, but the reset
cleared or overwrote it before anything could read it, and the ROM path
cannot read SRAM after a reset that boots V15.
