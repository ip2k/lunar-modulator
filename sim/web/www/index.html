Lunar Modulator

Intergalactic Modulation Station

An open firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, running in your browser. Its engines and effects are compiled to WebAssembly behind a to-scale FM-1 panel; the screen is the firmware's own 240 × 240 frame buffer.

Starts the audio. Then play the keys, your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard.

The panel is wider than this screen so its keys stay playable: drag the case sideways for the rest, or turn the phone to landscape.

Powered off.

Playing it

Keys
Click or touch; lower on a key plays louder. Computer keys A W S E D R F G Y H U J K O L P ; [ ' play the first 19 keys, F3 to B4.
OCT− / OCT+
Z / X. Both at once reset octave and transpose; hold one and turn ALGORITHM to transpose, as on the FM-1.
Encoders
Drag up or down, or scroll. ← → SELECT (page), ↑ ↓ PRESETS (sound), - = ALGORITHM (the sound's model, or the effect in FX mode). KNOB1–4 turn the four parameters on screen.
Buttons
FX shows the two effect slots (SELECT moves between them, SEL then SELECT swaps them), GLO the global page, HOME goes back. MASTER is the volume. Esc releases every note.
MIDI
Notes, pitch bend (±2 semitones), CC 7 volume, CC 123 all notes off.
Sequencer (lab)
A preview of work in progress. PLAY/STOP, or Space, starts and stops the sequencer, which holds a one-bar demo pattern on track 1. SEQ shows the track: the tempo, the transport and four bars of steps with the playhead; HOME, FX or GLO go back. In SEQ mode the white keys are the bar's 16 steps (also 1–8 and C V B N M , . /): tap one to add or remove a note, the last chord you played, or C4. Hold a step to edit it on the knobs: velocity, length, probability and condition; SELECT turns to invert. Hold A and press B to make A's note reach B. The OP1 and OP3 keys page bars, or nudge held steps; OCT transposes them. SEL (or Shift) is SHIFT: with a step held, the white keys add pitches and a tap of SHIFT alone clears the step; with none held, SHIFT and key 10 sets full velocity, and SHIFT with PLAY/STOP restarts. REC records what you play on the keys (outside SEQ mode) or at MIDI IN: stopped, after a bar's count-in. Hold REC in SEQ mode while stopped to enter notes one step at a time: the white keys play and enter their notes, keys held together make a chord, OP3 leaves a rest (or ties held keys into the next step), OP1 steps back, and SHIFT with a white key moves to that step. SHIFT and REC keep what you just played (Capture); stopped, it also reads your tempo and starts playing: SELECT tries another tempo, and any other press keeps the one you hear.
Sounds (lab)
Up to four sounds play at once. Hold SEL and turn PRESETS to choose the one the keys play and the knobs edit; PRESETS then picks its engine (Sounds 2–4 can be empty). In FX mode SELECT walks that sound's two insert effects, a Mix page with the four sounds' levels on the knobs, and the two master effects. The meter in the screen's bottom bar is the FM-1's memory: whatever would not fit is refused.

Panel to scale from the M-VAVE manual's dimensions (161.5 × 96.5 mm) and the owner's board photo; the encoders' printed names and the 14 buttons are the manual's.
