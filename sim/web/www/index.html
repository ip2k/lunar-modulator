Virtual FM-1

The open firmware's engines and effects, compiled to WebAssembly, behind a to-scale FM-1 panel. The screen is the firmware's own 240 × 240 frame buffer.

Starts audio. Then play the keys, or your computer keyboard.

The panel is wider than this screen so its keys stay playable: drag the case sideways for the rest, or turn the phone to landscape.

Powered off.

Playing it

Keys
Click or touch; lower on a key plays louder. Computer keys A W S E D R F G Y H U J K O L P ; [ ' play the first 19 keys, F3 to B4.
OCT− / OCT+
Z / X. Both at once reset octave and transpose; hold one and turn ALGORITHM to transpose, as on the FM-1.
Encoders
Drag up or down, or scroll. ← → SELECT (page), ↑ ↓ PRESETS (sound), - = ALGORITHM (the sound's model, or the effect in FX mode). KNOB1–4 turn the four parameters on screen.
Buttons
FX shows the two effect slots (SELECT moves between them, SEL then SELECT swaps them), GLO the global page, HOME goes back. MASTER is the volume. Esc releases every note.
MIDI
Notes, pitch bend (±2 semitones), CC 7 volume, CC 123 all notes off.

Panel to scale from the M-VAVE manual's dimensions (161.5 × 96.5 mm) and the owner's board photo; the encoders' printed names and the 14 buttons are the manual's. ENV, LFO, EDIT, SAVE, ARP, SEQ, PLAY/STOP and REC are not in the simulator yet. Engines credit Emilie Gillet (Mutable Instruments, MIT) and the Schwung module authors; see the repository's engines/README.md.
